First, the trains. I think a lot of people feel the same way as I do. In Japan, the trains and subways are fast, clean, and almost always on time. They can take you almost anywhere, so we didn’t need a car or a taxi. That’s also why the streets in Japan aren’t full of scooters like in Taiwan. With an IC card and Google Maps, getting around was very easy. This makes traveling on your own, or even backpacking, much easier in Japan.
