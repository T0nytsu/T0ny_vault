Hi everyone. Today our topic is music, but we’re not going to talk about which songs are good. We want to talk about how we listen.  

Here’s the whole story.
In the 1950s, records and radio kept music at home.  
And then, the Walkman and CDs came out, which let us carry it with us.  
In the 2000s, MP3s and the iPod put a thousand songs in our pockets.  
In the 2010s, we stopped buying songs and started paying for streaming.  
And today, a song can blow up in fifteen seconds, and AI can even make music.  
Music never left. What changed is the way we listen. So let’s start from the beginning: vinyl and the Walkman.
