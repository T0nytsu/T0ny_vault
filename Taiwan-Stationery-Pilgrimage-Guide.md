# 🗺️ The Taiwan Independent Stationery Pilgrimage Guide
### 台灣獨立文具店朝聖指南

*For people who don't shop for stationery — they disappear into it.*

---

## How this was built

This isn't a "top stationery stores" list pulled from memory. Every store below was cross-checked across PTT's 文具板, Dcard's 文具 forum, Threads/Instagram crowd-sourced maps, Taiwanese lifestyle blogs (熱血台中, YELLOWLEMON, 迷誠品, BIOS monthly, Pinkoi Zine, HereNow), and each shop's own Facebook/Instagram for current status. I prioritized stores that keep showing up independently across *multiple, unrelated* sources — that's the real signal of a cult favorite versus a one-off blog post.

**Explicitly excluded:** every chain you named (九乘九, 墊腳石, 光南, 金興發, 101文具天堂, 誠品, 博客來實體店, 金玉堂), plus a few borderline cases I cut on my own judgment — 菩品PROP (multiple locations, one already closed in 2024), 印花樂 (now a nationally-distributed design brand with too many storefronts to feel like a secret), and 尚羽堂 (a wonderful Taiwanese pen *workshop*, but its retail space is really a B2B showroom, not a browsing destination).

**One honest caveat:** these are one- or two-person-run shops. Hours shift with the owner's mood, restocks, and market schedules more than any chain ever would. Almost everyone below posts real-time hour changes on IG/FB — check the day before, always.

## PART 1 — The Top 20

### 1. 什物 Shiwu Studio — Tainan 🏆🎨
**97→101/120** · 💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Multiple independent bloggers, unprompted, call this "the most beautiful stationery store in Taiwan." It's a converted old house down an unmarked alley off Nanning Street, walled in by potted plants, with no sign worth the name — you find it the way you find a secret, by almost walking past it. The owner left a theater degree to chase this instead, and it shows in the pacing of the space: every corner is staged like a tiny stage set. It's also a working fountain-pen and ink shop, not just a pretty face.
- **Signature:** house-brand PET/washi tape collage series, Shachihata collab stamps, a genuinely huge houseplant collection
- **Best buys:** own-brand tape, postcards, seasonal stamps
- **Browsing time:** 1.5–2.5 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** anyone chasing the "secret world" feeling above all else; washi tape and plant people especially
- **Where:** 台南市中西區南寧街83巷32號 · hours shift — check FB before going, closed early week
- **Scores:** Browse 9 · Vibe 10 · Curate 8 · JP 7 · Tape 9 · Books 7 · Pens 6 · Local 8 · Display 10 · Wow 9 · Trip 9 · Lost 9 → **101/120**

### 2. 禮拜文房具 TOOLS to LIVEBY — Taipei (Da'an) 🏆📷
**97/120** · 💸💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Five graphic designers opened this, and it reads like a design studio that accidentally became a shop. No sign, no lightbox — just a always-open sliding door and a 100-year-old antique letterpress press sitting in the middle of the floor like a piece of sculpture. Monocle magazine found it early; Traveler's Factory in Japan now carries their signature clip design. This is the closest thing on this list to walking into a European stationer's shop from a hundred years ago, except everything is for sale and half of it is impossibly beautiful office supplies you never knew you needed.
- **Signature:** the antique press, wall of wood-type letters, BLACKWING pencils, vintage European/American brands (Coccoina glue, ZENITH staplers, DUX brass sharpeners)
- **Best buys:** BLACKWING pencils, brass desk objects, the house canvas tote
- **Browsing time:** 1.5–2 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** design-history nerds, people who want objects with a story, anyone who thinks a stapler can be beautiful
- **Where:** 台北市大安區樂利路72巷15號 · Tue–Sat 12:00–21:00, Sun to 19:00, closed Mon (also has a Kaohsiung 駁二 branch, see #24)
- **Scores:** Browse 10 · Vibe 10 · Curate 9 · JP 6 · Tape 6 · Books 8 · Pens 5 · Local 4 · Display 10 · Wow 10 · Trip 10 · Lost 9 → **97/120**

### 3. 愛治文具房 — Changhua 📒🎁
**97/120** · 💸💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Named after the owner's grandmother, run by two illustrator sisters, and repeatedly described by longtime Taiwan stationery bloggers as their single favorite shop in the country — the kind of place people fly home and go straight to. It's an official **Traveler's Notebook Partner Shop**, complete with an exclusive passport stamp, and the illustration/local-creator range here is the deepest on this whole list.
- **Signature:** TN Partner Shop stamp station, in-house illustration goods, huge rotating cast of Taiwanese indie brands
- **Best buys:** TN inserts, illustrated stickers and postcards, greeting cards
- **Browsing time:** 1.5–2 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** Traveler's Notebook and Midori people, sticker/illustration collectors, gift shoppers
- **Where:** 彰化市長安街76巷7-2號 · closed Mon–Tue, roughly 12:00–20:00 weekends — confirm on FB
- **Scores:** Browse 9 · Vibe 9 · Curate 9 · JP 6 · Tape 9 · Books 7 · Pens 5 · Local 10 · Display 8 · Wow 8 · Trip 9 · Lost 8 → **97/120**

### 4. 小品雅集 (TY Lee Pen Shop) — Taipei (Da'an) 🖋💸
**89/120** · 💸💸💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Founder 李台營 grew up unable to afford a decent pen and has since personally collected over 10,000 fountain pens; he calls what he does "pen diplomacy." The shop — two floors, an entire wall of ink — is sometimes called Taipei's answer to Itoya in Ginza, and it may genuinely be one of the largest dedicated pen retailers in the world. Staff will let total beginners test-write anything in the store with zero pressure to buy, right up until you somehow buy three pens anyway.
- **Signature:** the ink wall, rare/limited editions (an 18K solid gold Porsche Design pen among them), dip-pen bar in the basement
- **Best buys:** entry-level Japanese pens (Pilot, Sailor, Platinum), ink samples, dip pens
- **Browsing time:** 1–2 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** anyone who loves fountain pens, from total beginner to obsessive collector
- **Where:** 台北市大安區復興南路二段78巷76號1樓 · Mon–Sat 12:00–22:00, closed Sun
- **Scores:** Browse 9 · Vibe 8 · Curate 10 · JP 9 · Tape 2 · Books 6 · Pens 10 · Local 2 · Display 8 · Wow 7 · Trip 10 · Lost 8 → **89/120**

### 5. 嗨賴文具工作室 — Hsinchu ❤️
**89/120** · 💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

The only independent stationery select shop in Hsinchu — a city better known for semiconductors than stationery, which makes this place feel like an act of quiet rebellion. Owner 靜香 quit a stable engineering job at Hsinchu Science Park to run it; five years later, foreign tourists reportedly detour here on purpose. Resident shop cat 花花 outranks the owner in the FB bio, as is correct.
- **Signature:** wall of postcards mailed in by past customers, shop cat, workshop classes (watercolor, hard-pen calligraphy)
- **Best buys:** ink, washi tape, the seasonal commemorative stamp
- **Browsing time:** 1–1.5 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** cat people, anyone doing a Hsinchu HSR stopover, workshop-curious visitors
- **Where:** 新竹市北區江山街21號, near 城隍廟
- **Scores:** Browse 8 · Vibe 9 · Curate 8 · JP 7 · Tape 7 · Books 7 · Pens 6 · Local 6 · Display 8 · Wow 7 · Trip 8 · Lost 8 → **89/120**

### 6. 六街文具房 — Taichung (Beitun) 🎁
**87/120** · 💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Tucked into a residential block you'll walk past twice before finding it. The specialty is stamps — wood, concrete, sequential numbering, fully custom — alongside Sonny Angel, Smiski, and blind-box figures, plus genuinely unlimited free seating with no minimum spend, so you can sit and actually journal with what you just bought instead of rushing out.
- **Signature:** custom sequential/concrete stamps, huge stamp-pad wall, blind boxes
- **Best buys:** custom name/date stamps, blind-box figures
- **Browsing time:** 1.5–2 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** stamp collectors, gift shoppers, people who want to linger and write
- **Where:** 台中市北屯區安順東六街39號

### 7. 小徑文化 — Kaohsiung (Lingya) 
**86/120** · 💸💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Over 1,000 masking tape SKUs, tucked into an alley beside a small, aptly-named "Rainbow Park," a strip of greenery that regulars swear feels like a slice of Tokyo's Daikanyama transplanted to Kaohsiung. If you only care about one stationery category and that category is washi tape, this is the single deepest catalog on the island.
- **Signature:** 1,000+ tape catalog, house-brand tape line, the leafy alley approach
- **Best buys:** imported Japanese tape, own-brand tape
- **Browsing time:** 1.5–2 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** masking tape completionists
- **Where:** 高雄市苓雅區興中路一段435號1樓 · Tue–Sun 13:30–19:30, closed Mon

### 8. EMO Chill 選物文具房 — Taichung 📷
**86/120** · 💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Maybe**

### 9. 森活小室 — Kaohsiung (Xinxing) 💎📷
**85/120** · 💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

A PTT reviewer admitted they still get lost finding this place *after their third visit* — it's an old two-story house in a residential alley with genuinely no signage to speak of. Business cards are hand-cut with scissors. A vintage electric fan clatters by the door. There's a cat-shaped floor mat so realistic it's fooled more than one visitor. Everything the owner stocks, she picked herself, and when she restocks online first, the physical shelves can sell out before you arrive.
- **Signature:** hand-cut paper business cards, deep stamp selection, red-brick old-house photo corner
- **Best buys:** stamps, postcards
- **Browsing time:** 45 min–1.5 hrs (the space is genuinely tiny)
- **Who'll love it:** hidden-gem hunters, people who love a shop that fights you a little to find it
- **Where:** 高雄市新興區開封路301巷13號 · Wed–Sun 15:00–19:00, irregular
- **Scores:** Browse 8 · Vibe 9 · Curate 7 · JP 6 · Tape 7 · Books 5 · Pens 3 · Local 7 · Display 9 · Wow 9 · Trip 7 · Lost 8 → **85/120**

### 10. 有筆 × 鋼筆工作室 — Taichung (Xiqu)
**85/120** · 💸💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

The most complete one-stop shop in Taichung for both fountain pens *and* full TN/Midori/journaling supplies — a rarer combination than you'd think, since most shops specialize in one or the other. Extensive hand-written, hand-decorated sample pages throughout the store show exactly how every product looks in actual use, which turns "should I buy this" into "obviously yes."
- **Signature:** TRAVELER'S COMPANY & MIDORI wall, 1/1工作室 rubber stamps, huge fountain-pen test bar
- **Best buys:** starter fountain pen + ink set, TN inserts
- **Browsing time:** 1–1.5 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** people who want pens and journaling supplies in the same stop
- **Where:** 台中市西區長春街14號 · irregular days, check IG (roughly Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun afternoons)
- **Scores:** Browse 8 · Vibe 7 · Curate 8 · JP 8 · Tape 7 · Books 7 · Pens 8 · Local 6 · Display 7 · Wow 6 · Trip 6 · Lost 7 → **85/120**

### 11. 直物生活文具 + 直物文具Café — Taipei (Gongguan / Zhongxiao Xinsheng) 🌸
**84/120** · 💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Maybe**

Founder "Tiger" wrote 文具病 ("Stationery Sickness"), one of Taiwan's best-known stationery blogs-turned-book, before opening this five-*ping* (~17 sqm) time capsule of a shop in 2013. Every single item was personally used and vetted by him before it earned shelf space — you can find a 1950s Pentel Sign Pen once favored by architect Tadao Ando sitting next to century-old Japanese pencil-sharpening knives. The Zhongxiao Xinsheng branch adds Taiwan's first dedicated stationery café.
- **Signature:** historic/vintage functional design objects, house-brand stamp sets, cinnamon rolls at the café branch
- **Best buys:** classic Japanese/German pens, seal & stamp sets
- **Browsing time:** 1–1.5 hrs (plus café time)
- **Who'll love it:** design-object nerds, people who want a story behind every item, anyone who liked 禮拜文房具 but wants it more Japanese-leaning and more affordable
- **Where:** 公館店 台北市中正區羅斯福路三段210巷8弄10號 · Café Plain 台北市中正區新生南路一段56巷16之4號

### 13. 摸鼻子文房具 — Tainan
**84/120** · 💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Originally a stationery shop dating back to 1946, reborn by a new generation into something closer to a curated illustration gallery. The washi tape wall has every roll pre-unrolled as a visible sample, clearly labeled, *and* sold in small repackaged portions — genuinely rare generosity in a category built on FOMO-driven full-roll purchases.
- **Signature:** hard-to-find Taiwanese illustration brands, repackaged/sample tape system
- **Best buys:** repackaged tape samples, illustration stickers and cards
- **Browsing time:** 1–1.5 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** budget-conscious tape collectors, history-minded shoppers
- **Where:** near Tainan Art Museum (南美館) — confirm exact address and monthly open days on IG

### 15. 大人小學 古文具 — Taipei (Da'an) 📷
**83/120** · 💸💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Genuine antique stationery — old maps, mimeograph machines, decades-old office objects — paired with a café built around a deliberately theatrical ordering ritual: you pick a wooden token instead of talking to staff, drop it in a numbered slot, and wait. It's the single most novel, most "I have to show someone this" experience on the entire list.
- **Signature:** true vintage/antique stationery and ephemera, the token-ordering café system
- **Best buys:** genuine vintage stationery pieces, old maps
- **Browsing time:** 1–1.5 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** collectors of real antiques, people who love a novel ritual
- **Where:** 台北市大安區信義路二段86巷62號 · Fri–Sun 14:00–20:00
- **Scores:** Browse 8 · Vibe 9 · Curate 8 · JP 5 · Tape 4 · Books 5 · Pens 5 · Local 4 · Display 9 · Wow 10 · Trip 8 · Lost 8 → **83/120**

### 16. 美好文具室 — Taipei (Songshan)
**83/120** · 💸💸💸 · Worth special trip: **Absolutely**

Built around a genuinely great idea: a build-your-own notebook bar using TRAVELER'S COMPANY and Maruman's Kurutto Ring system, where you mix and match cover, pages, and binding ring until the notebook is entirely yours. A full wall lets you feel paper textures before buying, and every product has a staff-handwritten usage note taped beside it — this is the most beginner-friendly shop on the list without ever feeling dumbed down.
- **Signature:** custom notebook-building bar, paper-texture wall, staff sample decorating
- **Best buys:** a custom-built notebook, washi tape sample packs
- **Browsing time:** 1–2 hrs
- **Who'll love it:** notebook customizers, beginners who want hand-holding without condescension
- **Where:** 臺北市松山區民生東路四段100號1樓 · Wed–Sun 14:00–21:00, closed Mon–Tue

## 🏅 Special Awards

**🏆 Taiwan's Best Independent Stationery Store — 什物 Shiwu Studio.** The one store that shows up as someone's personal favorite across the widest range of unrelated sources, and the only one repeatedly described, unprompted, as "the most beautiful in Taiwan."

**🎨 Most Beautiful Store — 菓ノ文具曲.** A real Japanese-era wooden house, photographed best at golden hour, that earned its "most beautiful" nickname organically from local bloggers rather than its own marketing.

**📒 Journal Lover's Paradise — 愛治文具房.** The only official Traveler's Notebook Partner Shop on this list, with its own passport stamp and the deepest well of Taiwanese illustration/journaling goods anywhere.

**🖋 Fountain Pen Paradise — 小品雅集.** Two floors, a wall of ink, and a founder with a 10,000-pen personal collection. Nothing else on the island comes close in scale.

**🌸 Best Japanese Atmosphere — 直物生活文具.** Founded by the author of Taiwan's best-known stationery blog/book, curated entirely around the quiet, function-first Japanese design philosophy he wrote about for years.

**🎁 Best Gift Shopping — 六街文具房.** Custom stamps, Sonny Angel, blind boxes, and unlimited free seating to wrap things right there — the widest range of price points for gifting.

**💎 Best Hidden Gem — 森活小室.** A PTT regular admitted they still get lost finding it on their third visit. That's the bar for this category, and nothing else clears it as convincingly.

**📷 Most Instagrammable — 大人小學 古文具.** A wooden-token ordering ritual and genuine antiques in every direction — the most inherently shareable experience on the list.

**💸 Most Dangerous for Your Wallet — 小品雅集.** Stock ranges from NT$70 pens to a limited 18K solid-gold piece. One reviewer said flatly they've never once left empty-handed.

**✨ Store That Makes You Forget Time — 陸遛ㄦ文具所.** A visiting blogger described time itself seeming to stop while browsing the tape wall — about as literal a match for this award as you'll find.

**❤️ Store With the Most Passionate Owner — 嗨賴文具工作室.** Owner 靜香 walked away from a stable engineering career at Hsinchu Science Park to run this alone for five-plus years, cat co-manager included.

Notice what this list actually says: only two of these ten are in Taipei. If you've been treating Taipei as the whole of Taiwan's stationery scene, the real center of gravity is arguably Taichung and the Tainan/Kaohsiung corridor.

## PART 4 — The Ultimate Pilgrimage Route

**Philosophy:** this route follows Taiwan's High Speed Rail line from Taipei to Kaohsiung — which, conveniently, is almost exactly the north-to-south order this scene actually clusters in. But the *sequencing within each day* is built for emotional pacing, not efficiency: no more than three stores before a food break, the single biggest "temple" store of each day is never scheduled first thing (let your legs and your wallet warm up), and every day ends on a high note rather than a logistics scramble.

### Day 1 — Taipei, the Da'an temple crawl
Start at **小品雅集** right at opening, while staff have time to actually talk pens with you → short walk to **鋼筆工作室** to compare finds → lunch in Da'an → **品墨良行** for a slower, quieter paper-focused hour → **大人小學古文具** for the token-ordering novelty and a coffee reset → save **禮拜文房具** for late afternoon, when you're fed, unhurried, and ready for the best atmosphere on the list. Dinner in Yongkang Street, five minutes away.

### Day 2 — Taipei, the other side
**直物生活文具** (Gongguan) in the morning → **直物文具Café** (Zhongxiao Xinsheng) for lunch and coffee under the same roof as more of their curation → **美好文具室** (Songshan) in the afternoon to build a custom notebook while your brain is still fresh → optional evening wander through Dadaocheng for **立夏手帳生活** and a sunset by the river.

### Day 3 — Hsinchu stopover, then Taichung
Morning HSR to Hsinchu (35 minutes). **嗨賴文具工作室**, then lunch near 城隍廟 (the food stalls there are a destination in their own right). Back on the HSR to Taichung by early evening; check in, rest.

### Day 4 — Taichung, full day
**六街文具房** (Beitun) first, since it's slightly out of the main cluster → move into Xiqu district: **陸遛ㄦ文具所** → **有筆×鋼筆工作室** → lunch near 審計新村 → **EMO Chill 選物文具房** → save **菓ノ文具曲** for last, timed for the golden-hour light its wooden architecture is famous for. Dinner at 審計新村.

### Day 5 — Changhua, then Chiayi, then Tainan
Quick morning train to Changhua (15–20 min from Taichung) for **愛治文具房** → back to Taichung, HSR to Chiayi → **拾筆+鋼筆工作室** in the early afternoon → HSR onward to Tainan, arriving in time for dinner.

### Day 6 — Tainan
Unhurried morning at **什物 Shiwu Studio** — don't rush this one, it's the whole trip's emotional peak → old-town lunch and a wander through Shennong Street → **摸鼻子文房具** in the afternoon → evening in Anping for sunset and seafood.

### Day 7 — Kaohsiung, and the finish line
HSR or train to Kaohsiung. **小徑文化** → **森活小室** (budget extra time — you *will* get lost, that's part of it) → **禮拜文房具 駁二店** → **森町文具房** → end the whole pilgrimage at 駁二藝術特區 for sunset and a celebratory dinner.

### The condensed version (long weekend, 3 days)
If seven days isn't realistic right now: **Day 1** = the Da'an crawl above. **Day 2** = HSR down to Tainan for **什物** and **摸鼻子文房具**, stay overnight. **Day 3** = train up to Kaohsiung for **小徑文化** and **森活小室**, then HSR back to Taipei in the evening. You'll have hit 4 of the Top 5 and both Special Award winners for Most Beautiful-adjacent categories in one weekend.

**Two logistics notes:** most of these shops take Monday and/or Tuesday off, so don't build a route that lands on either day without checking first — and HSR Taichung Station is in Wuri, a 15–20 minute taxi from the Xiqu district stores, so budget that transfer time rather than assuming it's downtown-adjacent.
