## EUP §1.2 Measurements and Units

### EUP 1.17
A jetliner making a turn flies $2.1\ \mathrm{km}$ along a circular path of radius $3.6\ \mathrm{km}$. Through what angle does it turn?

### EUP 1.20
A year is very nearly $\pi \times 10^{7}\ \mathrm{s}$. By what percentage is this figure in error?

### EUP 1.26
Water leaves a tap at $15$ liters per minute. Express this flow in $\mathrm{cm^3/s}$ and in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$.

## EUP §1.3 Working with Numbers

### EUP 1.32
Add $5.1 \times 10^{-2}\ \mathrm{cm}$ and $6.8 \times 10^{3}\ \mathrm{\mu m}$, and multiply the sum by $1.8 \times 10^{4}\ \mathrm{N}$.

### EUP 1.33
Find the cube root of $1.25 \times 10^{23}$ without a calculator.

### EUP 1.35
An aircraft $41\ \mathrm{m}$ long gets a radio antenna $3.6\ \mathrm{cm}$ long on its nose. What length should a brochure give for the new model?

### EUP 1.36
Repeat EUP 1.35 with $41.05\ \mathrm{m}$ as the original length.

## EUP Chapter 1 Example Variations

### EUP 1.44
A comet's nucleus rotates with period $12.404\ \mathrm{h}$. Two years later the period has decreased by $21\ \mathrm{min}$. Find the new period in hours.

## EUP Chapter 1 Problems

### EUP 1.45
Find $(\sqrt{2})^{5}$ to three significant figures in two ways.
- (a) Round $\sqrt{2}$ to three significant figures, raise it to the fifth power, and round again.
- (b) Take $\sqrt{2}$ to four significant figures, raise it to the fifth power, and round to three significant figures.

### EUP 1.53
Bubble gum has density about $1\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$. A $7$-g wad is blown into a bubble $10\ \mathrm{cm}$ in diameter. Estimate the thickness of the bubble. The surface area of a sphere is $4\pi r^{2}$.

### EUP 1.55
A square chip $4\ \mathrm{mm}$ on a side contains $10^{10}$ electronic components.
- (a) How large is each component, if the components are square?
- (b) A calculation needs electrical impulses to cross $10^{4}$ components, each a million times. The impulses travel at about two-thirds the speed of light. How many such calculations can be done each second?

### EUP 1.65
Express each result with appropriate units and significant figures.
- (a) $1.0\ \mathrm{m}$ plus $9\ \mathrm{mm}$
- (b) $1.0\ \mathrm{m}$ times $1\ \mathrm{mm}$
- (c) $1.0\ \mathrm{m}$ minus $998\ \mathrm{mm}$
- (d) $1.0\ \mathrm{m}$ divided by $998\ \mathrm{mm}$

### EUP 1.67
In 2017 the solar panels on a house generated $3849\ \mathrm{kWh}$ of electrical energy, and the house consumed electrical energy at an average rate of $392\ \mathrm{W}$. Find the average rate of solar generation in watts, given $1\ \mathrm{kWh} = 3.6\ \mathrm{MJ}$ and $1\ \mathrm{W} = 1\ \mathrm{J/s}$. Did the house generate more or less energy than it consumed?

### EUP 1.69
For solid spheres of the same density, mass is proportional to volume, and $V = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}$. The table gives the diameter and the mass of five steel balls.

| Diameter (cm) | 0.75 | 1.00 | 1.54 | 2.16 | 2.54 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mass (g) | 1.81 | 3.95 | 15.8 | 38.6 | 68.2 |

- (a) Determine a quantity that gives a straight line when mass is plotted against it.
- (b) Make the plot, draw a best-fit line, and find its slope, which is proportional to the density.
