## ST §2.6 Implicit Differentiation

### ST 2.6.3
**1–4**
- (a) Find $y'$ by implicit differentiation.
- (b) Solve the equation explicitly for $y$ and differentiate to get $y'$ in terms of $x$.
- (c) Check that your solutions to parts (a) and (b) are consistent by substituting the expression for $y$ into your solution for part (a).

$$
\sqrt{x} + \sqrt{y} = 1
$$

### ST 2.6.5
**5–20** Find $dy/dx$ by implicit differentiation.
$$
x^2 - 4xy + y^2 = 4
$$

### ST 2.6.7
**5–20** Find $dy/dx$ by implicit differentiation.
$$
x^4 + x^2y^2 + y^3 = 5
$$

### ST 2.6.9
**5–20** Find $dy/dx$ by implicit differentiation.
$$
\frac{x^2}{x + y} = y^2 + 1
$$

### ST 2.6.15
**5–20** Find $dy/dx$ by implicit differentiation.
$$
\tan(x/y) = x + y
$$

### ST 2.6.17
**5–20** Find $dy/dx$ by implicit differentiation.
$$
\sqrt{x + y} = x^4 + y^4
$$

### ST 2.6.19
**5–20** Find $dy/dx$ by implicit differentiation.
$$
\sqrt{xy} = 1 + x^2y
$$

### ST 2.6.21
If $f(x) + x^2[f(x)]^3 = 10$ and $f(1) = 2$, find $f'(1)$.

### ST 2.6.24
**23–24** Regard $y$ as the independent variable and $x$ as the dependent variable and use implicit differentiation to find $dx/dy$.
$$
y\sec x = x\tan y
$$

### ST 2.6.25
**25–34** Use implicit differentiation to find an equation of the tangent line to the curve at the given point.
$$
y\sin 2x = x\cos 2y, \quad (\pi/2, \pi/4)
$$

### ST 2.6.29
**25–34** Use implicit differentiation to find an equation of the tangent line to the curve at the given point.

### ST 2.6.39
**37–40** Find $y''$ by implicit differentiation. Simplify where possible.

### ST 2.6.46
Show by implicit differentiation that the tangent line to the ellipse
$$
\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1
$$
at the point $(x_0, y_0)$ has equation
$$
\frac{x_0x}{a^2} + \frac{y_0y}{b^2} = 1
$$

### ST 2.6.48
Show that the sum of the $x$- and $y$-intercepts of any tangent line to the curve $\sqrt{x} + \sqrt{y} = \sqrt{c}$ is equal to $c$.

### ST 2.6.61
Find all points on the curve $x^2y^2 + xy = 2$ where the slope of the tangent line is $-1$.
