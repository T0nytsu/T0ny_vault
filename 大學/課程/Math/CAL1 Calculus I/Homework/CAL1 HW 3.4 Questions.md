### ST 3.4.58

$$

\lim_{x \to -\infty} f(x) = -\infty,\quad \lim_{x \to -2^{-}} f(x) = \infty,\quad \lim_{x \to -2^{+}} f(x) = -\infty, \\

\end{gathered}
$$
