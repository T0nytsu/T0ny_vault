### ST 3.5.59
Show that the curve $y = \sqrt{4x^{2} + 9}$ has two slant asymptotes: $y = 2x$ and $y = -2x$. Use this fact to help sketch the curve.

### ST 3.5.61
Show that the lines $y = (b/a)x$ and $y = -(b/a)x$ are slant asymptotes of the hyperbola $(x^{2}/a^{2}) - (y^{2}/b^{2}) = 1$.
