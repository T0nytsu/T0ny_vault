### ST 4.5.59
If $f$ is continuous and $\int_0^4 f(x)\,dx = 10$, find $\int_0^2 f(2x)\,dx$.

### ST 4.5.60
If $f$ is continuous and $\int_0^9 f(x)\,dx = 4$, find $\int_0^3 xf(x^2)\,dx$.

### ST 4.5.61
If $f$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$, prove that
$$
\int_a^b f(-x)\,dx = \int_{-b}^{-a} f(x)\,dx
$$
For the case where $f(x) \ge 0$ and $0 < a < b$, draw a diagram to interpret this equation geometrically as an equality of areas.

### ST 4.5.63
If $a$ and $b$ are positive numbers, show that
$$
\int_0^1 x^a(1 - x)^b\,dx = \int_0^1 x^b(1 - x)^a\,dx
$$

### ST 4.5.64
If $f$ is continuous on $[0, \pi]$, use the substitution $u = \pi - x$ to show that
$$
\int_0^\pi xf(\sin x)\,dx = \frac{\pi}{2}\int_0^\pi f(\sin x)\,dx
$$

### ST 4.5.65
If $f$ is continuous, prove that
$$
\int_0^{\pi/2} f(\cos x)\,dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} f(\sin x)\,dx
$$
